**Source-quality warning:** PDF page320 **physically duplicates printed catalogue entries 736 and 737**. It has conflicting old 737 *olim* footnote 36–1 vs36–2. More importantly, none of the period-specific earned/paid wage titles directly proves complete Jan–Dec1797 actual salaries. Do not calculate 1797 wage arrears from this finding aid.

## 4. Bank loan paper in the local treasury and the separate payment books

**Archive source discovery from PDF pages27–31 and290:**

- #134 p27 **Caja158 nº9**, 1794–1796, 7 communications: royal *vales reales* associated with Banco Nacional de San Carlos loan entering Vicente Oliag's Junta treasury.
- #139 p28 **Caja130 nº9**, 1795–1797: local treasurer correspondence and *cuaderno de pagos a los prestamistas* for construction. **Lender payments are not worker wages, and local Junta is not automatically Crown debtor.**
- #140 p28 **Caja118 nº15**, Feb–Apr1796: bank government paper entries and copy of note of vales.
- #141 p29 **Caja3 nº2**, Mar–Jul1796: treasury communications on vales deposited.
- #142 p29 **Caja56 nº17**, May1796–Dec1797: four communications on vales received from Banco San Carlos, same modern box as the January1797 roster nº19 but separate archival file.
- #144 p29 **Caja154 nº5**, Dec1796–Jan1797: two bank-paper entry communications.
- #135 p27 **Caja57 nº10**, Oct1794–Feb11 1797: treasurer correspondence with the date equal to historian's worker protest date Feb11 1797. **Coinciding endpoint is not proof this file discusses the unrest.**
- #152 p31 **Caja159 nº10**, year1798: consular duties *cargo y data* general statement, Banco San Carlos loans, receipts and payment authorizations. This could permit cross-account reconciliation after original pages.
- #565 p290 **Caja147 nº5**, full-year1797: 271 consular-dues payment orders; 271 is **count of orders, not nominal reales** and not proof of port-specific wages.
- For distinct competing controls, #727 p318 **Caja56 nº18** Jan1796–1797 prices of port materials and #733 p319 **Caja56 nº20** Feb20–Apr1 1797 daily incoming materials (59 folios). Nearby document physical sequence supports targeted request, but not claim of one integrated ledger.

**Numerical anomaly:** PDF p29 #143 **Caja89 nº20** describes Banco transfer as *500 millones*. This is incompatible in apparent scale with earlier 8.36 million nominal reales bank loan from published court transcriptions. It might be typo or different measure: **DO NOT ACCEPT 500 MILLION AS HISTORICAL BANK LOAN FACT** before original document and denomination review. Marked anomalous in source index.

Potential supplementary pre-loan archive #266 p236 **Caja85 nº2** (1793 port construction costs, Board meetings and receipts), #92 p19 **Caja108 nº4** (port works correspondence 1792–1801).

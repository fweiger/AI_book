## New data quality problem
1782 government original charter describes authorized 150000 shares x 2000 reales=300m capital. Banco official 2017 narrative says capital reduced to 240m in 1790, while FBBVA Table9.8 book capital remains 300m through 1794 and first reads 240m in 1795. Published bank history references 30628 own shares repurchased in 1785–89; later quoted 1806/1808 shareholder reports have 120000 outstanding shares. Different decision, economic and book recognition dates plausible but NOT VERIFIED. Mark contradictory year definitions, avoid false continuous year series.
